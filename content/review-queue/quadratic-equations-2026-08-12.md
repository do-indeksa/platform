## Recalculation notes

- `kv-004`: put $x_2=t$ and $x_1=t^2$. Vieta gives $t^3=8$, hence
  $(x_1,x_2)=(4,2)$ and $m=x_1+x_2=6$. Substitution factors the equation as
  $(x-2)(x-4)=0$.
- `kv-005`: solve $x_1+x_2=5$ and $2x_1-x_2=7$. This gives
  $(x_1,x_2)=(4,1)$, so $m=x_1x_2=4$; the stated relation becomes
  $2\cdot4-1=7$.
- `kv-006`: real roots require
  $D=-3m(m-4)\ge0$, so $m\in[0,4]$. The target is
  $g(m)=m^2-2(m^2-3m)=-(m-3)^2+9$, whose maximum on the interval is $9$ at
  $m=3$.
- `kv-007`: real roots require
  $D=4(m-4)(m+1)\ge0$, so $m\le-1$ or $m\ge4$. The target is
  $g(m)=4m^2-6m-8$. Its unconstrained vertex $m=3/4$ is outside the domain;
  $g(-1)=2$ and $g(4)=32$, while the function grows away from those boundary
  points. The global minimum is therefore $2$ at $m=-1$.
- `kv-008`: write the progression as $a-d,a,a+d$. The sum gives $a=5$;
  $3a^2+2d^2=83$ gives $d^2=4$. Both signs produce the same unordered set
  $\{3,5,7\}$.
- `kv-009`: write the positive progression as $b/q,b,bq$. The product gives
  $b^3=216$, hence $b=6$. The sum becomes
  $3q^2-10q+3=0$, so $q=3$ or $q=1/3$; both produce $\{2,6,18\}$.
- `kv-010`: on $x<1$, the equation reduces to $5-3x=5$, so $x=0$.
  On $1\le x\le2$, it gives $3-x=5$, whose candidate is outside the interval.
  On $x>2$, it gives $3x-5=5$, so $x=10/3$. Both retained values satisfy the
  original equation.
